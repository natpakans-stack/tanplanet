## MAJOR EVENTS THIS WEEK (Sep 7–13, 2026)

### 1. CHIP TARIFF PHASE TWO CONFIRMED — "BUILD IN AMERICA OR PAY" (Sep 3, 2026)
Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick confirmed at the G20 Innovation Ministerial that the Trump administration's Phase Two semiconductor tariff framework will follow a simple principle: **firms that manufacture chips in the US pay nothing; those that do not will pay to enter the market.** The preferred mechanism grants companies duty-free chip import allowances proportional to their investment in US semiconductor manufacturing. Phase 1 (25% tariff on advanced AI accelerators, effective Jan 15, 2026) is being expanded: **Phase 2 targets servers, laptops and gaming hardware**, and Phase 1 exemptions may not carry over. This is the clearest signal yet that the CHIPS Act reshoring investment cycle will be front-loaded and accelerating.  
*(Source: TechTimes "Chip Tariff Phase Two Confirmed: Build in America or Pay, Lutnick Announces" Sep 3, 2026; TechTimes "Trump Chip Tariff Phase 2 Targets Servers" Aug 28, 2026; TechTimes "Chip Tariff Phase 2 Lands as Fab Tax Credit Deadline" Aug 31, 2026)*

**Investment Impact:** Every new US fab requires roughly $10B in semiconductor equipment before producing a single chip. TSMC Arizona Phase 2 (3nm/2nm) equipment installation begins Q3 2026. Intel domestic fabs in progress. AMAT, LRCX, KLAC are the "picks and shovels" with pricing power and sole-source positions. The tariff acceleration also increases urgency for domestic AI data centers — reinforcing the Grid Power Delivery bottleneck (Theme 1 below).
