### 3. DIRECTED ENERGY WEAPONS — FIRST EVER US PRODUCTION CONTRACT (AVAV)

- LOCUST X3: 20–35+ kW directed energy system; defeats Group 1–3 UAS; engagement cost **~$13 per shot** vs hundreds of thousands of dollars per missile intercept.
- Expected deliveries: "well into the dozens" of LOCUST X3 systems to the Army.
- **September 8, 2026:** AVAV secured **first international commercial order** for LOCUST systems (valued >$50M) — marks global expansion milestone.
- Pentagon annual directed energy budget: ~$1 billion/year.
- Market size: $4.58B (2026) → $12.11B by 2035, CAGR 11.4%; High-Energy Laser segment at **16.6% CAGR**.
- Army Gauntlet II (mass production selection): 19 companies qualified from 49, testing 40 armed drones for mass production selection — manufacturing ramp now visible.

**Why now vs. prior Defense Autonomous theme:** Prior theme (KTOS/LMT/NOC) was about autonomous combat aircraft (CCA drone wingmen). Directed energy adds a **structurally different weapon system** category: infinite magazine depth (no reload), <$13/shot cost, counter-swarm capability. AVAV is the first and only company with a production DEW contract. KTOS has DEW work embedded in its revenue but not separately reported.
