### 3. AMAZON SIGNS X-ENERGY/AEP INDIANA SMR DEAL — 960 MW, 12 Xe-100 UNITS (Oct 19)

**October 19, 2026:** Amazon, X-energy, and American Electric Power (AEP) signed a **joint development agreement** to study a **960 MW small modular reactor facility** at the former Breed coal site in Sullivan County, Indiana. The plan calls for 12 of X-energy's Xe-100 reactors (80 MW each). Amazon is the development funder and would be the first power purchaser under a proposed long-term PPA.

This is Amazon's second SMR site with X-energy (following a Washington state facility) and represents the **fourth announced Xe-100 commercial project worldwide** within X-energy's ~11.5 GW commercial pipeline. Amazon has now committed to more than **5 GW of nuclear capacity by 2039** across multiple deals.

This is a feasibility-stage JDA (not a construction commitment), but it marks the first time a hyperscaler has publicly partnered with a **utility + SMR developer** in an integrated three-party structure, validating the AEP grid-interconnect + Amazon anchor-customer + X-energy technology model.

*(Sources: X-energy press release "X-energy, Amazon, American Electric Power Partner to Bring SMR Project to Indiana" x-energy.com / investors.x-energy.com Oct 19, 2026; Amazon Sustainability "Amazon invests in nuclear energy project targeting nearly 1 gigawatt" aboutamazon.com Oct 19, 2026; POWER Magazine "AEP, X-energy Eye 960-MW Nuclear Plant" powermag.com Oct 19, 2026)*
