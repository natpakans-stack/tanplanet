### 4. MARVELL ACQUIRES LUMENTUM FOR $115 BILLION — AI OPTICAL INTERCONNECT IS THE NEXT BOTTLENECK (Nov 2)

**November 2, 2026:** Marvell Technology (MRVL) announced an all-stock acquisition of Lumentum Holdings (LITE) at an implied enterprise value of **$115 billion**, representing a **~24% premium** to Lumentum's October 28 close. This is described by TechCrunch as "one of the largest deals in tech history."

**Strategic rationale (from Lumentum 8-K, SEC, Nov 2, 2026):**
- Combined total addressable market: **$550 billion**
- Run-rate cost synergies: **>$1 billion annually**
- Product scope: "scale-up and scale-in optics for AI data centers" — Marvell brings AI chip/ASIC design and electrical interconnects; Lumentum brings lasers, optical transceivers, and circuit switching.
- Nvidia is a shared major shareholder in both companies.
- Deal close: **H1 2028** (subject to shareholder + regulatory approval)

**Why this matters:** As AI data centers scale to 100,000+ GPU clusters, the electrical interconnect between GPUs must be replaced by optical fiber to handle bandwidth and reduce latency. The $115B deal is a direct signal that hyperscalers (and their silicon suppliers) regard **optical networking** as the structural bottleneck in the next phase of AI infrastructure. Pure-play optical networking stocks — Ciena (CIEN), Coherent Corp (COHR) — are the upstream beneficiaries not absorbed by the deal.

**Market reaction:** Marvell fell 13% and Lumentum dropped 19% on deal-dilution fears initially; MRVL then rallied +3.87% to $138.60 as strategists highlighted the TAM expansion.

*(Sources: Lumentum 8-K SEC filing sec.gov Nov 2, 2026; TechCrunch "In one of the largest deals in tech history, Marvell buys Lumentum for $115B" techcrunch.com Nov 2, 2026; CNBC "Lumentum CEO touts 'enormous' opportunity in massive AI connectivity deal with Marvell" cnbc.com Nov 2, 2026; Yahoo Finance "Marvell's $115-billion Lumentum acquisition" Nov 2, 2026)*
