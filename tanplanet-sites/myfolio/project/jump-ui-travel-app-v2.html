Project Overview

Jump (จัมป์) is a mobile app for travel, focusing specifically on prototyping solutions to address the given problem statement, as part of an internship selection project for Wongnai.

Duration: 5 days

Tools: photoshop, figma

‍

Context

Jump (จัมป์) is an app designed to deliver travel experiences that break away from the ordinary. It’ll guide you into a fresh, comprehensive style of travel—covering everything from stylish café-hopping to must-visit foodie hotspots acclaimed by travelers from everywhere. Additionally, it includes accommodations, whether you’re seeking a relaxing urban hotel or an adventurous escape into nature. With Jump, you can confidently pack your bags ahead of everyone else!

Logo

The name “Dorainme” sounds similar to the musical notes “Do-Re-Mi”, symbolizing musical instruments. As the app’s primary function is instrument rental, we creatively adapted “Re” to “Rent”, emphasizing the rental aspect in English. This play on words resulted in the name “Dorainme.”
