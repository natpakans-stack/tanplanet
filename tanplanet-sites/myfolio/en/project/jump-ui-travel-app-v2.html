Project Overview

Jump is a mobile app for travel, focusing specifically on prototyping solutions to address the given problem statement, as part of an internship selection project for Wongnai.

Duration: 5 days

Tools: photoshop, figma

Context

Jump is an app designed to deliver travel experiences that break away from the ordinary. It’ll guide you into a fresh, comprehensive style of travel—covering everything from stylish café-hopping to must-visit foodie hotspots acclaimed by travelers from everywhere. Additionally, it includes accommodations, whether you’re seeking a relaxing urban hotel or an adventurous escape into nature. With Jump, you can confidently pack your bags ahead of everyone else!
