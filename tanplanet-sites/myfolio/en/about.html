About me

I’m Natpakan Sirirat (Tan) — a Product Designer based in Thailand with a strong interest in using AI tools to enhance the design process. My design philosophy is simple: ask meaningful questions, then craft thoughtful, human-centered solutions.
‍
Curious by nature, I draw inspiration from philosophy, science, politics, and culture to enrich my design thinking — believing the best products come from understanding people, not just pixels.
‍
When I’m not designing, you’ll find me exploring new places through my camera lens or spending time with dogs. I’m drawn to projects with purpose — driven by empathy, curiosity, and a touch of joy.
