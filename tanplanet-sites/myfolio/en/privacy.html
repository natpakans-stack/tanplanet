Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

myfolio.tanplanet.work and projects.tanplanet.work are the personal sites of Natpakan Sirirat (Tan). This page explains what the sites collect, why, for how long, and how to ask for deletion.

1. Contact form

  • Collected: your name, a way to reach you (email or phone), your message, and the page you sent it from.
  • It is sent to my email through FormSubmit, and a copy is kept in my own back office.
  • It is used only to reply to you. It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing.
  • Kept for 12 months, then deleted. The IP address and browser name sent with the form are kept for 30 days to stop spam, then deleted.

2. Visitor counter

  • No cookies and no IP addresses are stored.
  • When a page opens, the site sends the page path, the referring site (domain only) and UTM parameters if any. Device type, browser and operating system are read from standard browser data.
  • Unique visitors are counted with an ID that changes every day, so you cannot be followed across days.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, you are not counted at all.
  • Kept for 13 months, then deleted.

3. Cookies and third parties

  • The sites use no tracking or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner.
  • Some fonts load from Google Fonts, and the sites are hosted on Vercel and GitHub Pages. These providers may see your IP address as part of serving files, under their own policies.

4. Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent through the contact form. Email natpakan.design@gmail.com and I will reply within 30 days.
